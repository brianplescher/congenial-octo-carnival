The work in three movements

Three threads.

Transformation. How individuals actually change — not in the self-help sense, but in the older, harder sense of becoming someone your former self would not recognize.

Collapse. What Anna Tsing calls the arts of living on a damaged planet. What Donna Haraway calls staying with the trouble. What Kropotkin, a century early, understood as mutual aid. I write in that lineage.

Practice. How to think, work, and build in a world where the old maps are useless and the new maps aren't drawn yet.
